## Current draft narration

In this course, consumers are people with relevant lived experience and expertise of a health condition or issue, including carers, who contribute their knowledge and perspectives to inform and shape innovation.

Being a patient or carer gives someone experience, but it does not automatically mean they are being involved in developing an innovation. What matters here is the role they are being invited to play: helping a team understand a need, question an assumption, consider options or make a decision.

The role of people with lived experience is not to become an overnight expert in science or technology – it is to bring a relevant and realistic perspective as a team member to make the concept or project even better than what it could have been.

It’s always important to consider whose lived experience voices are needed to inform the concept, the design and implementation and lastly, the delivery to ensure it can have the greatest impact/effect.

## Storyboard interpretation

The diagram distinguishes relevant experience from deliberate involvement.
Connections represent opportunities to inform and shape work, not automatic
membership, equal authority in every decision, or guaranteed impact.
The four examples are possible contributions, not a mandatory ordered process.
The question about whose experience is needed stays open across stages; the
diagram does not imply one person represents every relevant perspective.
