## Full sequence: RETProgramInformedConsent

1. Before joining a clinical trial, participants need to understand what they're
   agreeing to.
2. Informed consent is more than signing a form. It's an ongoing conversation
   about the trial's purpose, procedures, potential risks, benefits and alternatives.
3. Participation must be voluntary. Participants can ask questions, take time to
   decide, and withdraw at any time without penalty.
4. Ultimately, informed consent protects participants' rights and empowers them
   to make informed choices.
